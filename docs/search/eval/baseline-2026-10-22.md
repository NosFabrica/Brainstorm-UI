## The list previews

Places, Books and GitHub accounts on the Top page are matched in the browser over the items
the page loaded.

| List                  | Items on the relay | Items the page loads | Expected results in the pool | Matched there | Found by a relay probe |
| --------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------- | ---------------------- |
| Food and Drink Places | 7,954              | 969                  | 5 of 38                      | 3 of 38       | 9 of 38                |
| Books                 | 158,822            | 501                  | 0 of 5                       | 0 of 5        | 4 of 5                 |
| GitHub Accounts       | 7                  | 7                    | 1 of 1                       | 1 of 1        | 0 of 1                 |

Every one of the 1,000 places the page fetches carries the same `created_at` (one bulk
import), so "the newest 500" is whatever order each relay breaks the tie in: the search
relay's 500 and the hub's 500 share only 31 items. The same fetches repeated outside the run
return the same items: of the 54 Funchal places on the relay, 5 are loaded; of New York's 5,
none.

The relay probe sends the query as typed. It fails on the same words the Top page sections
fail on (see failures 1 and 6): asked by hand, `restaurants funchal` returns 5 Funchal
restaurants and `restaurants in funchal` returns none.

## Ten failures worth reading

1. **`restaurants in Funchal`**: no Places at all. Only 5 of the 54 Funchal places are in the
   969 the page loads, and the page matches whole words exactly, so "restaurants" never meets
   the category "Restaurant".
2. **`New York bar`**: PubKey NYC is not among the loaded places (no NYC place is). The relay
   ranks it 2nd when asked, but the Top page never asks the relay for list items.
3. **`bars in Funchal`**: People stays empty, as it should. Places is empty too: "bars" is not
   "Bar", and none of the four Funchal bars was loaded.
4. **`piranesi`**: the Books list holds 158,822 items and the page loads 501 of them (0.3%);
   Piranesi is not one. The relay ranks it 1st.
5. **`steakhouse in funchal`**: Central Grill is loaded, but its cuisine reads "Steak house".
   The page's matcher has no plurals, stems or synonyms.
6. **`what's on this weekend in chicago`**: no events. The relay requires every word, so
   "what's", "this" and "weekend" zero the section, and "this weekend" is never read as a time.
7. **`bird walk chicago`**: no events came back. The house lens drops the Chicago Bird
   Alliance calendar's publisher entirely: asking for that publisher's "bird" events returns 0
   under the observer and 5 under `include:spam` (probes outside the run). Korn at the Ziggo
   Dome (`concerts in amsterdam`) is lost the same way.
8. **`events in funchal`**: five events came back, all in the past. The section asks for the
   12 newest by publish time and Happening then drops everything that is over, so Saturday's
   BitcoinWalk Madeira never reaches the page. `bitcoin meetup this week` shows the same
   thing: 2 of its 10 shown events fall in the week.
9. **`events at fork & coin`**: four upcoming events, none at Fork & Coin. Loose matching
   brings back "Run For Hal", two Fort Worth conferences and a Bitcoin & Beers night; place
   precision is 0.
10. **`dune frank herbert`**: on the Top page a book matches by its title alone, so author
    words make even a loaded book unfindable. The relay ranks Frank Herbert's Dune 1st.

Also seen:

- `fork & coin hours`: "hours" becomes a word the place must contain. The place's own account
  is found for `fork & coin`; the place is not.
- `bitcoiners in chicago`: no people. Chicago Bitcoin Collective is found only by its name.
- `podcast`: the People section asked for 8 profiles and received 41. `limit` is not a ceiling
  (RELAY-ASKS #14).
- `amethyst` and `nostr clients`: the answer is apps, and the Top page has no Apps section.
- `ice cream in funchal`: the one absent-category violation, a single profile in People.

## What worked

- People by name: all 9 named-person lookups found the person in the top eight.
- Named events from the meetup calendar the house trusts: Plan ₿ Forum, BitcoinWalk Madeira,
  Bitcoin Amsterdam 2026, the Chicago Socratic Seminar, all in the top ten.
- Three place queries showed Places: `ice cream chicago` and `indian food in london`, whose
  words equal what the card shows ("Ice cream · Chicago", "Restaurant · Indian · London"), and
  `coffee in funchal`, which matched bakeries with "Coffee" in their names.

## About this run

- 132 requests (129 REQ, 3 COUNT), sequential, 1.5 s apart. Started 21:38 UTC.
- One relay error: the `amethyst` query could not connect three times (backed off 4 s, then
  8 s). It counts as a miss with no latency; its expected category is not on the Top page
  anyway.
- No drift between the runner's sections and the Top page's (index.html check).
- Time windows use America/New_York.
- The People counts are before the verified-line floor and near-duplicate collapsing, which
  the runner does not mirror; the page may show fewer.
