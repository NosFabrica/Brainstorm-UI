# Search baseline, 2026-10-25

The v1 query set (`queries.json`, 317 entries) run once against `wss://search.brainstorm.world`
through the house observer (`be7bf5de…`, from brainstorm.world's NIP-05), asking what the Top
page asks, as of app commit `96877028` (staging after the v1 set and the runner's update to the
app's Dictionary and whole-list search). 298 entries ran; the 19 `agent` entries did not. Raw
results: `baseline-2026-10-25.json`. How the runner works and what each metric means:
`README.md`.

Search finds people by name and finds topics. It now finds places when the words are the
words on the card, and misses them when they are not: plurals, abbreviations, question words.
It finds events only from native calendars: none of the 21 expected Event Index items reached
the top ten, because the Top page's events band does not ask for them. Queries that turn on a
judgement or on the reader's network mostly come back empty, and the social ones are slowest.

## Ten failures worth reading

1. **Event Index items never reach the Top page.** 21 expected answers are Event Index items
   (Luma, Mobilizon and other feeds), 20 of them in M3; none was in the top ten. The events band
   asks only for native NIP-52 events; only the Events tab reads Event Index items (#267). Native
   events fared better: 20 of 52 in M3.
2. **`restaurants in funchal`**: the relay returned 5 Funchal restaurants, and the page showed
   none, because "restaurants" never equals the category "restaurant". `burgers in san
francisco` and `steakhouse in funchal` fail the same way: the matcher has no plurals or
   stems.
3. **`bubble tea in sf`** and **`hackathons in sf this weekend`**: "sf" is in no item, so the
   relay returns nothing and the page shows nothing. `nyc coffee` shows no place and shows
   People instead; `tacos in LA` shows People beside its places.
4. **`who wrote neuromancer`** and **`1984 george orwell`**: no books. On the Top page a book
   matches by its title alone, so question words and author names zero the match.
   `lord of the rings tolkien` shows two books, neither Tolkien's.
5. **`what's on this weekend in chicago`**: still no events, as on 2026-10-22. "this weekend"
   is never read as a time, and every word must match.
6. **`will casarin`**: three people come back, none of them jb55, whose profile carries no real
   name.
7. **`reviews of lucky dawgs`**: nothing. "reviews" becomes a word the place must contain; the
   place is not found, and neither are the reviews.
8. **The long tail (M7) is nearly empty.** 9% of expected categories showed anything. Judgement
   words (cozy, romantic, dog friendly) and "football in funchal" zero the match.
9. **Social queries are slow.** M4's p95 is 11.3 s. The five slowest queries of the run are four
   social ones (`photographers my friends follow`, 14.7 s) and `golden gate bitcoin conference`.
10. **Apps.** 8 entries expect apps; the Top page has no Apps section, so they miss by
    construction.

## What worked

- People by name: navigational queries hit their category 86% of the time and found the person
  83% of the time, copycat profiles included (`preston pysh`, `hodlbod`, `kieran`).
- Topics: exploratory queries showed something in 82% of their expected categories; notes,
  articles, media and music sections nearly always answer.
- Places whose words are on the card: `pizza in funchal`, `sushi in lugano`, `tacos in austin`
  and `coffee in nashville` found every expected place.
- Named events on native calendars: `bitcoin walk funchal` found the weekly walk.

## About this run

- 1,316 requests (1,313 REQ, 3 COUNT), sequential, 1.5 s apart. Started 01:58 UTC on
  26 October (21:58 in New York on the 25th). Many more requests than v0's 132: each query now
  also asks the relay for list matches, as the page does.
- No relay errors, no timeouts, and no drift between the runner's sections and the Top page's.
- Time windows use America/New_York. The events entries were checked on 2026-10-24, so their
  weekend and this-week ids were current; they will expire, as `as_of` says.
- All 298 expected answers were found on the relay; a miss is search, not a stale id.
