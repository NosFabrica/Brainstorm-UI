# A6.1.i Resolve the Dictionary's concepts as Brainstorm does

A follow-up under A6.1, the Tapestry connector (nousclawds4/tapestry `docs/loom/A6.1.md`), found while building A6.1.e. This is its plan.

The connector's `tapestry_lists` reads a curator's headers, but not the Dictionary itself. A Claude asked what concepts exist has to guess who the curators are. Brainstorm's Dictionary page decides it in three steps (`hooks/useDictionaryConcepts`):

1. **The house:** the house observer, the `_` name in Brainstorm's NIP-05 (`services/trustSource`).
2. **The curators:** the house's taggings with the curator tag, through `curatorsOf`.
3. **The concepts:** the curators' self-pointing headers, through `conceptsOf`, less the concepts that play a role, such as URL Templates.

`curatorsOf` and `conceptsOf` are already in the list module. The curator tag and the role concepts aren't. They are in `config/dictionary.config.json`, read through `config/dictionary`, which also reads the tag relays from the env and the browser, so the module doesn't import it.

## What it changes

**In this repo:** `listTools.ts` exports two more values from the JSON itself, beside `URL_TEMPLATES_LIST` (A6.1.h):

- `CURATOR_TAG`, the tag that makes someone a curator;
- `ROLE_CONCEPTS`, the concepts the Dictionary keeps out of its view.

The build test checks both against the config, and checks that `curatorsOf` and `conceptsOf` answer as the source does.

**In Loom (nousclawds4/loom):**

- `scripts/pin-lists.ts` re-pins to this change's commit.
- `tapestry_lists` gains `read: "dictionary"`:
  - It finds the house from `https://brainstorm.world/.well-known/nostr.json?name=_`, as the app does on its own origin.
  - It asks the relays for the house's curator taggings and the curators' headers, with the app's filters.
  - It resolves them with the pinned `curatorsOf` and `conceptsOf`, and drops the role concepts.
  - It answers the house, the curators, the concepts in the Dictionary's order, and each concept's header, whole and signed.
- If the house can't be found, or no relay answers, it says so rather than answering an empty Dictionary.

## Done when

`read: "dictionary"` on the live connector gives the concepts brainstorm.world's Dictionary page shows, in the same order.
