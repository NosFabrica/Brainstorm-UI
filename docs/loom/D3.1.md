# D3.1 Write the rebuild test

The spec for a decision task on the search roadmap. The decision itself is
[ADR 0009](../adr/0009-linkers-view-rebuilt-from-the-brain.md).

## Problem

The linker (E2) will read meaning from a precomputed view of the graph: filed headers, their schemas and enumerated
values, labels and aliases, and edges by type. The roadmap's D3 says an instance's brain, its Neo4j graph plus the
Tapestry LMDB, is where truth lives, and that the linker's view is a cache the Duality engine derives from it and
Vespa holds. Nothing yet says what that has to mean in practice: what a fresh instance starts from, what it may not
read, and what happens when the view falls behind the brain. Left open, whoever builds E2.1 decides it, and "run your
own Brainstorm" has no test.

## For whom

Whoever builds the linker's view (D3.2), the projection job (D3.3) and the linker (E2), and anyone who runs their own
Brainstorm.

## Done when

An ADR in `docs/adr/` that:

- states the rebuild test in the instance's own terms (brain, derived views, events read and written): what a fresh
  instance starts from, what it runs, what passes;
- says what counts as NosFabrica's brain, as against its relays, which are relays like any other;
- says what "a stale view never changes a result" asks of the linker;
- says where today's code and data stand.

It replaces the first draft's "rebuildable from relay data alone". The decider merges it.

## Out of scope

The view's fields (D3.2), the projection job (D3.3), where the linker runs (D3.4), the freshness policy (D3.5),
published or derived edges (D5), and per-observer trust (D6).
