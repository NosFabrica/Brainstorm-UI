# A6.13.d The labelled set's agent queries, answered through the tool

A story under A6.13, the interim search tool (NosFabrica/brainstorm-connector `docs/loom/A6.13.md`). This is its plan.

The labelled set (`docs/search/eval/queries.json`, E6.1) has 19 `agent` entries: the structured form of a question, as
an agent or a query parser would send it, which the baseline runner skips. This story answers them the way a teammate's
Claude would today, through `brainstorm_search` and the guides, and scores each answer against its expectation.

## How they are answered

- **By Claudes that haven't seen the expectations.** Fresh Claudes, given each entry's structured question and, where its
  notes give them, the words it stands for, but never `queries.json` or anything under `docs/search/eval/`.
- **With the connector's tools:**
  - `brainstorm_search`, the connector's own code at `f962c43` (after A6.13.c's fixes), run against
    `wss://search.brainstorm.world` as one signed-in teammate (observer `b83a28b7…`);
  - `tapestry_lists`;
  - the guides, starting with `ui/search/find-me` and `ui/search/syntax`.
- **On one day:** Saturday 2026-10-31, the reader in America/New_York. "This weekend" is 31 October and 1 November.
- **What each answer gives:** up to ten results with their brainstorm.world links, the searches it ran, and what it
  said was missing.

## How they are scored

The baseline's metrics, where they fit an answer:

- **Category hit:** the answer has at least one result of the expected category. Places are items of Food and Drink
  Places, and events are calendar events (31922, 31923) or Event Index items.
- **Recall@10:** the share of expected ids among the answer's results.
  - Only ids still right on the day count. An event that has passed is dropped from the expectation and named.
  - An entry with no id left is scored without recall.
- **Filter precision@10:** read from each returned item's own tags: a place's `locality`, `category` and `cuisine`, and
  an event's location and start against the window.
- **A verdict for each entry:** answered, partly, or not. Each miss gets one cause:
  - **the tool:** what `brainstorm_search` can't ask or doesn't return;
  - **the guide:** what `ui/search/find-me` or `ui/search/syntax` left the Claude to guess;
  - **missing data:** what isn't on the relay.
