# E6.1 Write the labelled set

The spec and story breakdown for an epic on the search roadmap: growing the
[labelled query set](../search/eval/README.md) from v0 to the 300 to 500 queries the roadmap asks for.

## Problem

Every later search change is judged by the labelled set (`docs/search/eval/queries.json`) and the runner that scores
search against it. Set v0 has 93 queries. [ADR 0008](../adr/0008-first-search-journeys.md) decided how the set leans
on the first journeys:

- about 30% places recommendation (M2) and about 20% events (M3);
- every demo city has queries in both;
- both have `agent` entries;
- the other journeys keep their entries;
- results are reported per journey.

v0 meets the shares (28 and 18 of 93) but is too small to measure a change with confidence, and it is lopsided:

- no places or events query names San Francisco or Nashville;
- 11 of the 28 places queries are in Funchal;
- 34 of the 93 entries have no checked expected results.

## For whom

Everyone who changes search: the linker in the relay (E2), the answer page (E4), and anyone reading the harness's
numbers to decide whether a change helped.

## Done when

- `queries.json` holds at least 300 entries in v0's schema, with the shares and coverage above.
- Every id in an entry's `results` was looked up on `wss://search.brainstorm.world` (the runner's `on_relay` check
  passes), and every entry has `notes`.
- A test in the repo's own suite (`npm test`) fails when the shares drift or a demo city loses its coverage.
- The baseline is rerun on the grown set, with results reported per journey.

v0's entries stay, including the staging demo cases (E6.2). The demo cities are Chicago, New York, San Francisco,
Austin, Funchal and Nashville.

## Out of scope

Running the harness in CI (E6.4), targets for the metrics (E6.3), query logging (E6.5), and changes to how the runner
scores an answer.

## Stories

### E6.1.a Places recommendation queries (M2)

- kind: build

Grow the M2 entries from 28 to about 100. Every demo city gets at least five, San Francisco and Nashville included, and
cities the list is dense in (Los Angeles, Lugano, San Salvador) get some. Cover cuisine, category and near me, the
"best X in Y" shape of the demo query, and cuisines with more than one value since Mise en Place keeps them all. Add
at least five `agent` entries. Where the list has no answer (brunch in Chicago has none today), leave `results` out
and say why in `notes`. Done when the M2 entries number 95 to 105 and the runner's `on_relay` check passes for every
new id.

### E6.1.b Events queries (M3)

- kind: build

Grow the M3 entries from 18 to about 65. Every demo city gets at least three, and the time words in the README
(today, tonight, this weekend, this week, next 7 days, this month, next 30 days, upcoming) are each used at least
twice. Mix Event Index items and native NIP-52 events, and add at least five `agent` entries. Events expire, so every
entry with `results` carries `as_of`. Done when the M3 entries number 60 to 70 and every new id passes `on_relay`.

### E6.1.c Navigational and lookup queries (M1)

- kind: build

Grow the M1 entries from 21 to about 65: named people, places, books, apps and events (navigational), and facts
about one of them such as opening hours, an address or an author (lookup). Prefer entries with one right answer and a
checked id. Done when the M1 entries number 60 to 70 and every new id passes `on_relay`.

### E6.1.d Social, exploratory and long-tail queries (M4, M5, M7)

- kind: build

Grow M4 (social), M5 (exploratory) and M7 (long tail and meaning) from 26 entries together to about 95, roughly a
third each, with at least one `agent` entry in each. Social answers depend on the reader, so most of these will carry
categories and filters without `results`; `notes` say whose network an expectation assumes. Done when the three
journeys together number 90 to 100.

### E6.1.e Hold the shares and rerun the baseline

- kind: build
- depends_on: E6.1.a, E6.1.b, E6.1.c, E6.1.d

Add a test to the repo's suite that reads `queries.json` and fails when:

- an entry breaks the schema, or two entries share an `id`;
- M2 falls outside 25 to 35% of the set, or M3 outside 15 to 25%;
- a demo city has no M2 or no M3 entry;
- M2 or M3 has no `agent` entry.

Update the README's counts, then rerun the baseline on the grown set and commit its results, reported per journey and
per class. Done when `npm test` runs the check, and the new `baseline-<date>.json` and `.md` are in
`docs/search/eval/`.
